## Motifs
- **The TOMORROW card:** every future notification uses one design (dark glass card, a coral
  "TOMORROW · 7:02 AM" tag, a sender and a preview). It's the visual signature of the video.
- **Blue phone glow** on Maya's face = the power. The room stays dark; the phone is the key light.
- **Tally mark** in the corner of the lock screen = "tomorrow keeps count" (a seed for the cost later).
- **Red clock 11:59** = the ritual.
