# What If Every GUY on Earth Loved You?

You look at the guy guarding you, pick up basketball, down by one, five seconds left. You've got the ball and he's right in your face. He stares at you. Then he drops his arms, steps forward, and hugs you. You shoot over his shoulder. Swish. Nobody on his team even gets mad. They're in line for a hug, too. Every guy on Earth loves you. Not likes, loves. About 3 billion grown men. And it never turns off. Until one day, 3 billion of them are chasing you across the whole planet. The problem? You like girls. Only girls. But right now, it's day one. And right now, it's just one guy who won't let go. Bro, your eyes are amazing. Thanks. Can you let go? No. See, a normal guy meets you and thinks, "He seems fine. Maybe you're friends in a few months." These guys, one look, one word, even one photo, that's all it takes. When you have a crush on someone, your heart beats faster, your hands get sweaty, your brain fills up with a feel-good chemical, the same one you get when you win. For every guy who sees you, that feeling is cranked all the way up, every single time. And it never fades. And at first, there's some upside. Your friends start making bets. Bet you can't beat the strongest guy in the gym at arm wrestling. He's 300 lb. He looks at you and slams his own hand down. Then he cries a little. Bet you can't win at go-karts. 12 drivers. Every one of them pulls over to let you pass. One of them blows you a kiss. A pro football team signs you after one video. They've never seen you play. The coach just says, "I need him." A pro boxer fights you for charity. You swing, you miss by a foot. He falls down anyway and stays down. The pizza guy brings you 40 pizzas. You ordered one. You win every bet, every game, every time. But, there's a catch. They don't just want to give you stuff. They want to date you. Week one, 50 guys follow you home from the store, then 500, every one of them holding flowers. Your mailbox fills up with love letters, then your yard. One guy shaves your face into the side of his head. Your best friend comes over to play video games. He doesn't play. He just stares at you. Bro, have you always smelled this good? You say it every way you can. I'm not into guys. I like girls. Please stop. They don't hear it. He's playing hard to get. And the girls you like, they can't even get near you. There are 200 guys in the way. One girl waves from the back of the crowd and laughs, a lot. It's not that guys are the problem, it's that 3 billion of them won't take no for an answer. Week three, guys line up outside your door to propose. The line is 6 miles long. A thousand guys get your face tattooed on their chests. A billionaire buys a blimp. It flies over your house every morning. Marry me on the side. Somebody makes an app, where is he? It tracks you live. 300 million downloads in a week. There's nowhere to hide. So, you run. You learn to get out of anywhere. Back window, over the fence. You slide under a closing garage door. 20 guys slam into it. Skateboard down the steepest hill in town. 40 guys on scooters behind you. You jump onto a moving bus. Wave goodbye, then the driver looks in the mirror. He loves you, too. He drives you straight back. You try disguises, fake beard, sunglasses, a wig. They find you in seconds. It's him. I'd know that walk anywhere. You move cities. They follow. You move countries. They're already there. You rent a boat, sail out to the middle of the ocean. Nobody for a thousand miles. You wake up and there are 2,000 fishing boats around you. Every one of them is blasting the same love song. So, you go to the emptiest place on Earth, Antarctica. The whole continent has only about a thousand people in winter. You step off the plane and most of them are guys. Every one of them is holding a cup of hot chocolate. You run so much, your body changes. A normal person's heart beats about 70 times a minute at rest. A pro athlete's, about 50. After 3 months of running from guys, yours is 45. You're in the best shape of your life, but you never sleep. After 24 hours awake, your brain is about as slow as a drunk driver's. You fall asleep standing up, in closets, on roofs, in a dumpster once. And then, the guys stop going to work. Guys do about six out of every 10 paid jobs on Earth. In America, about nine out of 10 construction workers are guys. Nine out of 10 truck drivers, almost every electrician. So, buildings stop going up. Trucks stop moving. Power lines go down and nobody fixes them. Every sports league shuts down. The players just want to watch your live stream. But, some things get better. In America, about eight out of 10 people arrested for violent crimes are guys. Now, crime drops off a cliff. It's hard to rob a bank when you're busy following one guy around. The news calls you the most wanted man on Earth. Kings and presidents send you letters with hearts. Three countries offer to make you their king and there's a cost. Your friends aren't your friends anymore. They're fans. Your best friend hasn't looked you in the eye in a month. He just stares at your hair. You can't go to a game. You can't get a burger. You can't just hang out. Every guy on Earth loves you and you've never felt more alone. Then, one night you get a message from a girl. "Hi, I'm a scientist. I think I can help. Also, I don't love you at all." It's the nicest thing anyone said to you in months. Dr. Nina runs a lab full of virus scientists, every one of them a woman, which means they can be in a room with you and actually get work done. They sneak you in inside a pizza box, a really big pizza box. "Sit down. Don't talk. You're distracting the security guard." They test everything. Your blood, your sweat, your voice. They bring in a guy volunteer. Try one. They show him a blurry photo of your elbow. He proposes to the photo. Try two. A voice changer. You sound like a robot. "Hello, I am normal." He loves the robot. Then, Zoe, the youngest scientist on the team, figures it out. When guys see you, their brains make way too much bonding chemical. That's the stuff that makes people want to stay close, and it gets stuck. Scientists have blocked that chemical before in tiny animals called prairie voles. Normally, they pick one partner for life. Block the chemical and they don't pick anyone. So, the girls build a blocker. Version one gives the volunteer hiccups for a day. Version 12 works. He looks at you, shrugs, "Nice hoodie, I guess." and walks out. You almost cry. But, it's one tiny bottle and 3 billion guys. So, Nina has an idea. A cold. A cold spreads on its own. One sneeze, one handshake, and it jumps to the next person. Scientists have already built germs that spread a vaccine by themselves. They tested one on wild rabbits on an island in Spain. So, the girls build a cold that carries the cure. A runny nose for a day, then the love is gone. But, how do we get it to every guy on Earth? Nina looks at you. "Easy. Every guy on Earth is trying to get close to you." They give the cold to you. It can't make you sick, but anyone who gets close catches it. For months you've been running away. Now you need them to catch you. Day 100, the world soccer final. Almost 90,000 fans in the stadium, more than a billion watching at home. Fans from all over the world who all fly home tomorrow. Halftime, you walk out of the tunnel onto the field. The whole stadium goes quiet. Then 90,000 guys stand up at once. Both teams turn around and they all start running toward you. Zoe's voice in your ear. Wait, one more thing. Some germs can jump to animals. We don't know if this one can. The tunnel is right behind you. Open. Your way out, like always. In front of you, 90,000 guys. If you run, nobody catches the cure and they never stop chasing you, ever. If you stay, nobody knows what happens next. 5 seconds. Nobody else on Earth can make this choice. Only you. You stop running. You turn around and open your arms. They catch you. Thousands of hugs. The goalkeeper lifts you off the ground. Somebody sneezes. Then everybody sneezes. The next morning the fans fly home. About 100,000 flights take off every day and the cure is on all of them. In 2009 a new flu reached more than 70 countries in about 2 months. The cure does it in 3 weeks. One by one guys wake up. Runny noses, blinking. Why is he on my chest? The blimp lands. The app gets deleted. The trucks start moving. The lights come back on. The guy from that first pick up game finds you. Sorry about the hug, man. Rematch? This time he blocks your shot, easily. You've never been happier to miss. Your best friend comes over, picks up a controller, and beats you, badly. Then laughs in your face. It's the best day of your life. Nina shakes your hand at the lab. Most people would have run for that tunnel. You turned around. Then a dog follows you home. Then another. Then every dog on your street. Pigeons land on your windowsill. Dozens of them staring at you. Your phone rings. Zoe. So, remember the animal thing? There are almost a billion dogs on Earth and they're not the biggest problem. Your phone buzzes again. Unknown number. We can make it stop. Come to the zoo alone. Outside, every bird in the city lands on your street and they all turn to look at you.
