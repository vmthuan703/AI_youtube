And meat is a slow way to make food. A cow eats about 25 lb of plants to make 1 lb of beef. Right now, almost 80% of the world's farmland is used for animals. Their fields and the crops grown to feed them. But they give us less than 20% of our food. So, when everyone goes vegan, you skip the middleman. You just eat the plants. The crops that used to feed animals, now they feed people. Scientists figure that's enough food for about 4 billion more of us. And most of that land, nobody needs it anymore. Your muscles don't care where the the comes from. You need about 60 g a day. One cup of lentils has 18. Tofu, beans, peanut butter, it adds up fast. 12 weeks later, your bench goes from 135 to 165. Some of the strongest fighters, sprinters, and strongmen on Earth were already vegan. They just didn't talk about it this much. And everything around you changes fast. Burger places switch to plant patties overnight. The menu looks the same. The cows are just gone. Thanksgiving. Americans usually eat about 46 million turkeys. This year? Zero. Your family carves a giant stuffed squash. Your uncle takes a picture of it. Nobody knows why. Super Bowl Sunday. That's normally about 1 and 1/2 billion chicken wings. This year? It's cauliflower. Pizza's different. Plant cheese melts. It just doesn't stretch. You pull a slice, no string. Nothing. Your dog is fine. Dogs can live on plants. Your cat? Cats need stuff that's only in meat. So, scientists make it in a lab. Your cat sniffs the new food and walks away. And here's the weird part. Eating together gets easier. Nobody's the picky one anymore. Any table, any country, everyone can eat everything on it. But not everyone is happy. Around the world, more than a billion people make a living from animals. Ranchers, fishermen, butchers. Your grandpa raised cattle for 40 years. Overnight, nobody's buying. New jobs show up. Bean farms, plant cheese factories. Just not where grandpa lives. He sells the trucks. He keeps the cows as pets. But where it gets crazy is the land. Without animals to feed, about 3/4 of all farmland goes empty. That's an area as big as the US, China, Europe, and Australia put together. And nature takes it back. Right now, if you weighed every mammal on Earth, the wild ones would be just 4%. The rest is us and our farm animals. 5 years in, that starts to flip. Grass grows over the pastures, then bushes, then young forests. And every new tree pulls carbon out of the air. Deer come back, bison. Then the wolves follow the deer. Your town gets a new road sign, wolf crossing. Your friends dare each other to walk home after dark. But no hunters means way more deer. And deer love roads. America already has over a million deer crashes a year. Now, there are more. Your mom hits one on the way to school. And here's another problem. Cows used to eat the dry grass. Now, it just piles up. Hot dry summer, and that grass becomes fuel for bigger wildfires. And the farm animals? There are about 25 billion chickens alive right now. Nobody breeds new ones. So, in 10 years, chickens get rare. Some old breeds of cows and pigs vanish for good. The oceans change, too. People pull more than a trillion fish out of the sea every year. Then, zero. Fish don't need forests. They just need time. Within 10 years, many fish populations bounce back. Whales get more food. Seals pack the beaches. And sharks follow the seals. That already happened once, on Cape Cod. The seals were protected. They came back and the great whites came right behind them. Then the air starts to change. Farm animals make about 1/7 of all the gases heating up the planet. That's about as much as every car, truck, ship, and plane on Earth combined. A big part of that is cow burps. One cow burps out around 200 lb of methane a year. Methane traps heat way better than car exhaust, but it fades fast. In about 12 years, most of it is gone. So, when the burps stop, the warming slows down faster than almost anything else people could do. The water gets cleaner, too. Every summer, farm runoff makes a dead zone in the Gulf of Mexico. Thousands of square miles where fish can't breathe. Now, it starts shrinking. And superbugs? Most of the antibiotics sold in America go to farm animals. That's how germs learn to beat them. Now, they don't get the practice. And fewer animal farms means fewer new flus jumping from animals to people. The world notices. Rivers run clearer. Towns that used to smell like pig farms just smell like towns. Fast-food chains go to war over who makes the best fake bacon. And the hottest job on Earth? Food scientist. Your body changes, too. Scientists think a vegan world could prevent about 8 million deaths a year by 2050. Fewer heart attacks, less diabetes. But, there's a catch. One vitamin, B12, is almost impossible to get from plants. It's made by bacteria. Without it, your body slowly breaks down. Tired, dizzy, numb hands. So, now the whole world takes a tiny pill or eats cereal with it added in. Skip it for a few years and you're in trouble. And some people can't farm plants at all. Herders in deserts, families in the Arctic, their land grows grass or nothing. For them, going vegan isn't a diet, it's survival. Even your sneakers change. No leather, so more fake leather made from plastic. And there's a cost. Grandpa's cows get old, one by one. Then one morning, the barn is just empty. Every summer, you two used to go fishing at the lake. Now the rods sit in the garage. He never asks to go again. At dinner, he still sets out a steak knife every night out of habit. And sometimes, at a cookout, the grill hisses. Smoke. That smell. And for one second, you remember. 10 years later, Grandpa's ranch is a forest now. Elk walk through his old barn.
